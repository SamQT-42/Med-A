# Med-A — 90-second demo script

**Before you start:** `npm run dev`, open http://localhost:5173, click
**Reset demo** in the sidebar. Screen at 1366×768 or larger.

One sentence of framing, then drive. Times are cumulative.

---

### 0:00 — Frame it (10s)

> "Med-A turns a work difficulty into a specific work adjustment that has an
> owner, a response time, and a review date. Everything you'll see is fictional
> data in a prototype — the badge at the top says so."

Point at **Prototype · fictional data** and **Scripted demo**.

### 0:10 — The difficulty (15s)

On **Today**, click **Load Alex example**.

> "Alex has three urgent requests and doesn't know where to start. Note the text
> is only *filled in* — nothing has been sent."

Click **Continue**.

> "The assistant names the *work* barrier, not a person. It asks one question:
> has your manager said which comes first? Alex says no."

Click **No**.

### 0:25 — Consent is the product (25s)

Click **Prepare a request for clear priorities**.

> "Here's the editable draft. It's a proposed workplace experiment, not a medical
> prescription."

Click **Continue to review**. Slow down here — this screen is the pitch.

> "Before anything is shared: who gets it, how, when a response is expected,
> when it's reviewed. Then the exact text — and, just as importantly, what is
> **not** shared. The private chat, any diagnosis, any screening data, and every
> private rating Alex records later."

**Optional 8-second flourish if you have time:** expand *Change the wording
without leaving this screen*, type a word, and show the share button going grey.

> "Change the request after approving it and the approval stops applying. Only
> the version you actually reviewed can be shared."

Re-review, then click **Share this request with Sam**.

### 0:50 — The manager (20s)

Switch **Demo view → Manager — Sam**.

> "Sam sees the approved operational request. No chat, no health information —
> and Alex's disclosure is not hidden here, it was never copied into this object."

Click the **↓** next to *B — Budget revision* so the order reads **A → C → B**.

> "Sam decides the business priorities. Med-A doesn't invent deadlines or choose
> them for him."

Click **Use the demo note**, then **Record response**.

### 1:10 — Did it actually change anything (20s)

Switch back to **Employee — Alex**, open **My support plan**.

> "Alex sees the agreed order and the first step. But notice the status:
> *manager agreed*, not *delivered*. Only Alex can confirm the information
> actually reached them."

Click **Yes, it was available**.

Click **Simulate five working days later** in the sidebar.

> "That's a presentation control — the badge says simulated day 5. No real time
> passed."

Set **Yes**, **Better**, **Keep**.

> "These ratings are private by default. Sharing just the decision needs its own
> separate preview."

### 1:30 — Land it (10s)

> "That's the loop: difficulty, request, consent, response, delivery confirmed by
> the employee, review. One fictional response doesn't prove efficacy — and the
> app says so rather than claiming an ROI number."

## If someone asks…

| Question | Answer, and where to click |
| --- | --- |
| "What if they don't want a chatbot?" | **Talk with a person** on Today — reachable without chatting or any test. Open it. |
| "Does the employer see who's neurodivergent?" | **Organization — overview**. Operational counts only. The cohort panel says *Not enough participants to display outcomes* at 4 people. |
| "What if the AI fails mid-pitch?" | Tick **Simulate AI failure**, click Continue. Nothing is sent, the text survives, **Use scripted example** recovers. |
| "Is this clinically validated?" | No. The Library cards say *Public guidance summary; partner review pending*, and Support options say *Partner details to be added*. |
| "Can they take it back?" | **Withdraw in-app access** on the support plan — and read the line beside it, which says it can't undo what someone already read. |

## Recovery

Anything goes wrong: **Reset demo** in the sidebar clears everything and returns
to a fresh Today. It takes one click and about a second.
